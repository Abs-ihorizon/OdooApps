Odoo Withholding Tax, Bulk Payment and Currency Rate Solution

Affinity Withholding Tax and Currency Rate Management is an Odoo Accounting extension for vendor bills, customer invoices, WHT deduction, payment reconciliation and currency rate management. It helps accounting teams process vendor payments and customer collections individually or in bulk while automatically calculating the applicable withholding tax according to configured tax rules.

The application supports full and partial payments, multi-invoice payment processing and proportional WHT calculation. Users can enter the reconciled amount, review the tax deduction and payment breakdown, and keep invoice status and outstanding balances updated in Odoo.

End-to-End Process

A structured workflow from tax configuration to payment reconciliation.

STEP 1

Configure Withholding Tax

Create the required WHT percentage by defining its name, description, percentage, tax type and scope. The rule can be configured for goods or services and mapped to the relevant withholding tax account.

STEP 2

Apply WHT on Bills and Invoices

Create a vendor bill or customer invoice, select the product or service, enter quantity and price, and apply the required WHT percentage. The corresponding WHT amount is calculated for review before the document is posted.

STEP 3

Select Single or Multiple Documents

Select one or multiple bills or invoices using the list-view checkboxes, then click Pay to initiate the settlement process.

STEP 4

Choose Payment Details

Select the relevant bank journal, payment method and payment mode. For an installment or partial settlement, choose Partial Payment.

STEP 5

Enter Reconciled Amount and Calculate WHT

Enter the partial amount against the relevant document. The application calculates the applicable WHT from the configured tax rule and displays the allocation before payment creation.

STEP 6

Review Payment Breakdown

Review invoice totals, outstanding amounts and the reconciled value for each selected document before creating the payment.

STEP 7

Track Updated Status and Amount Due

After processing, Odoo automatically reflects the new payment status and remaining amount due, providing a clear audit trail for partially and fully settled documents.
